Intruder Alarm Systems

The right alarm for your home — installed properly.

A domestic intruder alarm is the single most effective deterrent against residential burglary. Most opportunistic burglars will move on when they see — or hear — clear signs of a professional alarm installation. But a system only works properly if it's specified correctly for your home and installed by an engineer who knows what they're doing.

We install wired, wireless and hybrid alarm systems from Pyronix, Texecom and HKC — three of the most respected names in the UK residential security market. Each brand has its own strengths, and we'll recommend the right one based on your property type, construction and what you want the system to do. A newer build with cavity walls lends itself to different solutions than a period property or a large detached house with outbuildings.

All our residential alarms are installed to BS EN 50131, the European standard that governs intruder alarm equipment and installation quality. Graded systems under this standard are the type your insurer is likely to specify — so you're not just getting peace of mind, you may also qualify for a reduction in your home insurance premium. We'll always confirm what grade is appropriate for your property and provide full documentation for your insurer on completion.

Our Installation

What's included with every alarm installation.

Free Home Survey
Before quoting, one of our engineers visits your property to assess every entry point, room layout and any specific vulnerabilities. You get a written, itemised quote based on what your home actually needs.
System Design
We specify the right control panel, sensor types and siren positions for your property. Passive infrared detectors, door and window contacts, shock sensors and vibration detectors are all selected based on your layout.
Professional Installation
All cabling is run neatly and out of sight where possible. We work tidily, protect your floors and furnishings, and don't leave until we've done a full clean-up.
Full System Test
Every zone, sensor, siren and keypad is tested in full before we leave. We walk you through the system, show you how to arm and disarm it, and answer any questions.
BS EN 50131 Certification
You receive a completion certificate confirming the grade of installation. This is the document your insurer needs — and we'll advise you exactly what to send them.
24/7 Engineer Support
Once your system is live, we're available around the clock. If the alarm activates unexpectedly, a sensor develops a fault or you need to change user codes, we're a phone call away — not a call centre.
