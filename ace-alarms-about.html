Certification & Standards

What our SSAIB certification
means for you.

The SSAIB — Security Systems and Alarms Inspection Board — is the leading independent certification body for security and fire system installers in the UK. Being SSAIB certified isn't something you can self-declare. It requires an independent audit of our work, our engineers and our business practices, carried out on a regular basis.

For you as a customer, it means two practical things. First, your insurer will recognise our installation — most home and business insurers require or prefer an SSAIB certified installer for any alarm or fire system to count towards your cover. Second, it gives you independent recourse if anything ever falls short — the SSAIB holds us accountable, not just our own word.

All our installations reference the relevant British Standards — BS EN 50131 for intruder alarms and BS 5839-1 for fire detection systems. Compliance certificates are issued on completion of every installation.
