Multi-Site Contracts

Your whole portfolio. One contract. Zero chasing.

If you manage security across multiple premises, you know the real cost isn't the systems — it's the administration. Different installers at different sites, certificates in different formats, renewal dates scattered across the calendar, and a different phone number to ring depending on which building has the fault. When an auditor or insurer asks for evidence, someone loses a day pulling it together.

A multi-site contract with Ace Alarms replaces all of that with one agreement covering every site — fire alarms, intruder systems, CCTV and access control, whoever originally installed them. We survey each site, bring the documentation up to a single standard, and schedule all maintenance visits so nothing lapses anywhere. Every site is serviced to the same British Standards, by the same team, with consistent reporting in one format.

You deal with one dedicated account contact who knows your whole portfolio — and when a fault occurs at any site, your priority SLA applies across all of them, 24/7. As your portfolio changes, sites are added to or removed from the contract without renegotiating from scratch. It's how managing agents, retailers and care groups across Sussex and Surrey keep dozens of buildings compliant without it becoming someone's full-time job.

Contract Inclusions

What's included in every multi-site contract.

Portfolio Survey & Onboarding
We visit every site, record what's installed, assess its condition and gather or recreate the missing documentation. You get a clear baseline report on the whole portfolio before the contract starts.
Consolidated Maintenance Schedule
All fire, intruder, CCTV and access control servicing across all sites planned on one schedule, at the frequencies the relevant standards require. We track the dates — nothing lapses, nowhere.
Consistent Standards Site-to-Site
Every site is serviced by the same team, to the same British Standards, regardless of who originally installed the system. Quality and compliance stop depending on which building you're standing in.
Unified Documentation
Certificates, service reports and asset registers in one consistent format, organised per site and per portfolio. When an insurer or auditor asks, the evidence is a single email away.
Priority Response — Every Site
Your SLA applies across the whole portfolio, with engineers on call 24/7. One number to ring for any site, answered by people who already know the building and its systems.
Dedicated Account Contact
A single named contact who manages your contract, knows your sites and handles additions, removals and upgrades as your portfolio changes. No re-tendering every time something moves.
