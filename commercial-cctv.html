Commercial CCTV

Full-site surveillance that earns its keep every day.

Commercial CCTV works hardest when nobody is watching it. It deters break-ins and antisocial behaviour, settles disputes about deliveries and incidents, supports insurance claims with clear evidence, and lets managers check any site without leaving their desk. The difference between a system that delivers all of that and one that produces unusable footage is in the design and installation.

We install Dahua HD and IP camera systems — a platform trusted by security professionals worldwide for consistent image quality and reliable hardware. Coverage is planned camera by camera: entrances, tills, loading bays, yards and perimeters, with resolutions up to 4K where identification-grade footage matters. Footage records to a local NVR you own and control — no mandatory cloud subscriptions — with retention periods configured to your policy.

For managers and multi-site operators, remote viewing is set up as standard: live and recorded footage from every site, from one app, with individual user permissions so staff only see what they should. And because workplace CCTV carries data protection obligations, we'll advise on signage, siting and retention under UK GDPR so your system protects the business in every sense.

Our Installation

What's included with every commercial CCTV installation.

Site Survey & Coverage Plan
We walk the site with you and map every camera position against what you actually need to see — entrances, tills, stock areas, perimeters. You approve the coverage plan before any work begins.
Professional Camera Installation
Weatherproof, vandal-resistant cameras fixed at heights that maximise coverage and deter tampering. All cabling is run neatly through containment and clearly labelled for future maintenance.
NVR Setup & Retention Policy
Your recorder is configured with the correct recording schedules, motion zones and retention periods for your data protection policy — so footage is there when you need it and gone when it shouldn't be kept.
Remote & Multi-Site Viewing
Live and recorded footage on desktop and mobile, across one site or your whole portfolio. Individual user accounts mean managers, supervisors and directors each see exactly what they're authorised to see.
GDPR & Signage Guidance
Workplace CCTV must comply with UK GDPR. We advise on camera siting, signage and retention as part of every installation — so your system stands up to scrutiny from staff, visitors and regulators alike.
Handover, Training & Support
We train your team on live viewing, playback and exporting footage for police or insurers. After handover, our engineers remain available for faults, adjustments and system expansion as your business grows.
