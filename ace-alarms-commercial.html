Standards & Compliance

We work to the standards
your insurers and auditors expect.

SSAIB certification isn't just a badge — it means every system we design and install is independently audited against nationally recognised standards. For commercial clients, that translates directly to insurance compliance, tender eligibility and regulatory peace of mind.

BS 5839-1
Fire Detection & Alarm Systems
The benchmark for commercial fire alarm design, installation and commissioning. Required by most commercial insurers and building regulations.
BS EN 50131
Intruder & Hold-Up Alarm Systems
The European standard for graded intruder alarm systems. Specifies the grade and security level required for your premises type.
SSAIB
Security Systems & Alarms Inspection Board
Third-party certification body. Our SSAIB registration means your systems are covered for insurance purposes and police response.
Compliance Certificates
Every installation comes with full documentation and certificates issued on the day of handover.
Annual Inspection Reports
Maintenance contracts include annual inspections with full written reports for your records and auditors.
Priority SLA Response
Commercial clients receive a priority response SLA — your business doesn't wait behind a residential queue.
Dedicated Account Contact
One point of contact who knows your sites, your systems and your service history.
