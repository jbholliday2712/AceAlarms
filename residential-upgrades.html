System Upgrades

A modern alarm system — without starting from scratch.

A lot of homes have an alarm system that was installed ten or fifteen years ago and has never been touched since. These systems may still make a noise if triggered, but they're often missing the features that make a modern alarm genuinely useful — smartphone control, instant push alerts, wireless sensors and proper grade compliance. They may also be out of support from the original manufacturer, with spare parts no longer available.

In many cases, upgrading doesn't mean pulling out everything and starting again. Existing wiring can often be reused, which keeps costs down and minimises disruption to your home. We'll assess your current installation — cables, sensors, panel positions — and tell you honestly which parts are worth keeping and which need replacing. In some properties, a completely fresh installation is the better option; in others, a panel swap and new detector heads is all that's needed.

For homes where the original wiring is in poor condition or access is difficult, wireless upgrade kits from Pyronix, Texecom and HKC allow us to fit new sensors without running any new cables at all. These systems are fully graded, insurance-approved and include all the same app connectivity as a wired installation. We'll walk you through the options at the survey and give you a clear comparison so you can make the right decision for your home and your budget.

The Upgrade Process

What's included with every system upgrade.

Free System Assessment
We visit and assess your existing installation — panel type, cable routes, sensor positions and overall condition. We'll identify what can be reused and what needs replacing, then give you a written quote before any work is agreed.
Wiring Reuse Where Possible
Where your existing cable runs are in good condition, we use them for the new system. This avoids unnecessary disruption and keeps costs down — particularly important in homes with plastered ceilings or decorative finishes.
New Control Panel
A modern Pyronix, Texecom or HKC panel replaces your old one. New panels include full app connectivity, improved zone management and current firmware support — meaning your system will continue to receive updates and remain supported for years to come.
Wireless Upgrade Option
Where running new cables isn't practical, we fit wireless sensors that communicate directly with the panel. These are fully graded, insurance-approved devices — the same brands and standards as any hardwired installation, just without the cables.
App Setup & Configuration
Your new system is configured for full smartphone control from day one. We set up the app on your devices, walk you through arming, disarming and alert settings, and make sure everything is working before we leave.
Full Test & BS EN 50131 Certificate
Every zone, sensor and siren is tested in full once the upgrade is complete. We issue a new BS EN 50131 installation certificate which you can provide to your insurer — confirming your upgraded system meets the current standard.
