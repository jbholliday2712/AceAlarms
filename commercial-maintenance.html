Maintenance Contracts

Compliance isn't a one-off event. It's a contract.

A fire or intruder system is only compliant on the day it's commissioned. From that point on, the standards — and your insurer — expect it to be maintained: BS 5839-1 recommends fire alarm inspections at least every six months, and most insurers require graded intruder systems to be serviced at least annually. Miss those visits and you risk invalidated cover, failed audits and licence conditions you can't evidence.

Our commercial maintenance contracts put all of that on a schedule you never have to think about. We plan the visits, carry out the inspections, replace consumables like standby batteries before they fail, and issue full written reports and certificates after every visit — the paper trail your insurer, fire risk assessor and auditors will ask for. You also get a priority callout SLA, so a fault at your premises is answered ahead of the general queue, by engineers available 24/7.

We maintain systems we installed and systems originally installed by other companies. If your current provider has disappeared, become unresponsive or simply isn't keeping your documentation in order, we can take over the contract, survey what's there and bring everything up to date — usually without replacing any hardware.

Contract Inclusions

What's included in every commercial maintenance contract.

Planned Preventative Maintenance
Scheduled inspection visits at the frequency the relevant standard requires — six-monthly for fire systems, at least annually for intruder. We book them, we track them, you just receive the reports.
Fire Alarm Servicing — BS 5839-1
Panel checks, detector testing on rotation, sounder and call point tests, battery and fault-log inspection — all recorded in your log book with a service certificate issued on every visit.
Intruder & Security Servicing
Every detector, contact and siren tested; signalling and monitoring paths verified; standby batteries load-tested and replaced as needed. CCTV and access control can be wrapped into the same contract and visit.
Priority Callout SLA
Contract clients receive a defined response SLA, with engineers on call 24/7, 365 days a year. Your business doesn't wait in a general queue — and out-of-hours faults are handled by people who know your system.
Compliance Reports & Certificates
A full written report and certificate after every visit, plus an annual inspection summary for your records. When your insurer, auditor or licensing officer asks for evidence, it's already in your file — and ours.
Dedicated Account Contact
One named contact who knows your sites, your systems and your service history. No re-explaining the building to a stranger every time something needs attention.
